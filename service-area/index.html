Town by town

What sets each nearby town apart

Water behaves differently depending on where it comes from. These notes explain why, so you know what to tell the dispatcher.

Spanish Fork, ZIP 84660

The Spanish Fork River runs through the city, and the city floodplain map shows FEMA zones A, AE, AO and shaded X, so check the current map for your address. A state flood report in April 2023 listed Spanish Fork Canyon among active areas of concern.

Spanish Fork water damage

Salem, ZIP 84653

The Salem Canal, completed in 1869 and about 8 miles long, carries Spanish Fork River water, and the city has grown by about 32 percent between 2020 and 2025. That growth means many newer homes, so tell the dispatcher your home's age and where the water is coming from.

Salem water damage

Santaquin, ZIP 84655

Slopes east of town burned in the 2001 Mollie fire, and debris flows hit homes there in September 2002. Most Santaquin homes are fairly recent, and the city's 2023 runoff page reported canyon flows at 3 to 4 times normal at the peak.

Santaquin water damage

Springville, ZIP 84663

Hobble Creek runs through the city, and the city notes that owners along the creek clear their own banks. Many homes sit in the Springville Historic District, with older construction types such as adobe and bungalows.

Springville water damage
Dispatch

How coverage and dispatch work

Every call goes to the same 24/7 line, (385) 988-9410. The dispatcher asks where you are, what the water is doing and whether anyone is at risk. If there is any electrical, gas or fire danger, you will be told to call 911 first.

Next, a crew lead explains what the visit will involve, including the scope and price before work starts. Arrival depends on the day, the weather and the crews available, so we do not promise a window. We do offer same-day response when available.

Storm days and spring runoff can mean many calls at once. Calling early, even before water reaches the worst of it, helps the dispatcher plan.

Which service page fits your call

Every town on this page is served from the same 24/7 line, and the right page depends on what happened. Burst, frozen and appliance lines are covered under frozen pipe water damage and appliance leak water damage. Wet basements and under-floor water are on the basement and crawl space pages, and heavy weather is on storm water damage.

If you are not sure, call and describe what you see. The services page groups every option by cause, room and material.
