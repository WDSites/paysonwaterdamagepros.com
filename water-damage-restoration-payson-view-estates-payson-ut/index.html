What the planning record describes

The minutes of the January 24, 2024 Payson City Planning Commission meeting describe Phase 4 of the Payson View Estates Subdivision as east of Main Street between 1800 South and 1900 South. The request covered 22 detached single-family lots on four Utah County parcels, with lots from 0.20 to 0.39 acres and an automobile bridge over the Highline Canal.

The commission held a public hearing and voted to recommend approval of the preliminary plan for that phase. This page does not state what has been built since, and the minutes say nothing about the earlier phases. Homes differ, so ask about yours.

Detached homes, more exterior to look after

Detached single-family homes on lots of this kind have more outside plumbing than a townhome: hose bibs, sprinkler zones, valve boxes and lines running along the foundation. A split hose bib or a stuck sprinkler valve can send water toward a wall for hours before anyone notices.

Inside, the usual suspects are the same as anywhere. A water heater fitting, a dishwasher hose or a washing machine supply line can leak quietly into flooring. Call (385) 988-9410 and describe what you found, and the next step is explained on the phone.

The canal and your irrigation water

Payson City Public Works and the Utah state water plan list the Strawberry High Line Canal among the sources of the city's pressurized irrigation water. That is a supply fact about irrigation, and nothing in it suggests the canal threatens homes.

What it does mean is that irrigation water arrives on a seasonal schedule, so a leak in a sprinkler line often shows up when the system is turned on in spring. Shut the zone off if you find soggy ground near a wall. A plumber or irrigation contractor repairs the line.

How a wet house is dried

The steps depend on what the readings show.

Water removal

Standing water comes out first with extraction equipment, which stops it from soaking further into flooring and walls.

Moisture mapping

Meters and a thermal camera find wet areas that look dry, such as the base of a wall or a subfloor.

Air movers and dehumidifiers

Moving air and dehumidification pull moisture out of the structure, with readings checked until materials reach a dry standard.

Cleanup and rebuild support

Wet materials that cannot be dried are removed, and the area is cleaned so a contractor can rebuild.

Safety when water comes from outside or above

Roof, snowmelt and exterior leaks add a few hazards to the usual ones.

Do these in order

  1. Keep out of wet areas with live power. Do not use breakers or electrical equipment while standing in water, and keep people and pets away.
  2. Shut the water off if it is safe. Use the hose bib, zone or main valve only if you can reach it without wading through water.
  3. Stay off a wet or icy roof. A roofer handles the source, and ceiling water can be photographed from inside.
  4. Call for immediate danger. For sparks, a gas smell or a sagging ceiling, leave and call the utility or 911.
  5. Photograph when safe. Pictures of the source and the wet surfaces can help later.

Contaminated water

Sewage or contaminated floodwater is a professional job. Do not use fans or household vacuums on it.

Factors that move the scope

Every job is different. These factors tend to decide it.

  • How long the water sat before it was noticed
  • Whether it entered from the roof, wall or floor
  • How many rooms and layers of material are wet
  • Whether the water was clean or contaminated
  • Access to wet cavities and crawl areas, if the home has them

Photos can help document visible damage, moisture readings can support a restoration record, and an itemized scope can describe the work. Check your policy and talk to your insurer, because coverage depends on the cause and the policy terms.

Roof water and winter melt

Payson City's 2023 flood-preparation letter reported snowpack at Payson Canyon SNOTEL at 253 percent of normal in April 2023, with high water generally peaking from mid-May to mid-June. That describes snowmelt in the canyon, not any particular street.

At the house, the question is smaller: did melting snow find a way past flashing or into a wall? See roof leak water damage for ceilings, and water damage cleanup for what follows. Water in a garage or lower area is covered by water removal.
