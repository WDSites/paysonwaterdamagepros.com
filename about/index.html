About Payson Water Damage Pros

What this business is

Payson Water Damage Pros is a Payson, Utah emergency line for water damage and mold. When you call, calls are answered 24/7 and the dispatcher sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to your property in Payson or in a nearby town.

We serve Payson (ZIP 84651) and the neighboring towns of Spanish Fork, Salem, Santaquin and Springville. Our office is at 39 N Professional Way, Payson, UT 84651.

This page skips founding stories and job counts, because neither helps you decide whether to call. What helps is knowing how the work is done and what to check.

How a call works

You call (385) 988-9410 and describe what you see. The dispatcher asks about safety first, then about the source, the rooms affected and how long the water has been there.

A crew lead explains the next step, including the scope and price before work starts. We offer same-day response when available but do not promise an arrival time.

  • Safety first: electrical, gas or fire risk means 911 before anything else
  • Describe the source, the rooms and the materials affected
  • Scope and price are explained before work begins
  • Photos, moisture readings and drying logs support your claim

What a job looks like from start to finish

Most water damage jobs follow the same arc, and knowing it helps you judge any company you call. First comes safety and source control, which means stopping the water, checking for electrical hazards and deciding what must not be touched. Then comes extraction, where standing water is removed before anything else is done.

Drying is the longest stage. Crews place air movers and dehumidifiers, take moisture readings on day one and check them again until the materials reach a dry standard. Skipping the readings is how hidden dampness turns into mold weeks later, so ask to see them.

Cleaning and removal run alongside drying. Materials that cannot be dried safely, such as saturated carpet pad after contaminated water, are taken out and replaced. When the structure is dry, repairs such as drywall, paint and flooring can start, and that part may be handled by us or by a contractor you choose.

Throughout the job you should receive plain answers about what is being done and why. If a company cannot explain its drying plan or will not show moisture readings, that is a reason to ask more questions before you sign anything.

The standards the trade follows

Two published industry standards shape professional work. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 is the Standard for Professional Mold Remediation. The water categories 1, 2 and 3 and the damage classes 1 to 4 come from S500.

We do not claim any credential on this page. Ask any restoration company you consider, including us, whether its crews work to S500 and S520, and ask how they apply them on a job like yours.

How to verify any contractor

Hiring after a loss is stressful, and that is exactly when checking matters. These steps work for any company.

Before you hire

  • Look up the licence. You can look up a contractor on the state's DOPL licence lookup, run by the Division of Professional Licensing.
  • Ask about insurance. Ask the company to confirm it carries insurance, and ask what it covers.
  • Get the scope and price first. A written or clearly spoken scope before work starts prevents disputes later.
  • Ask about mold licensing. For mold work, ask whether your state licenses mold work.
  • Ask about standards. Ask whether crews work to S500 and S520.

What we do not do

We are not a waterproofing contractor, a plumber or an insurer. Restoration deals with damage after water enters, so foundation cracks and drainage problems need the right trade. We do not give medical advice, so health questions about mold go to a physician. We do not decide your claim, so check your policy and ask your insurer.
