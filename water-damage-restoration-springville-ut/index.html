Springville's older houses leak in older ways

The Springville Historic District was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in January 2004. It counts 1,238 primary buildings, about 72 percent of them contributing, and the styles include adobe homes, Victorian Eclectic, bungalows, period revival and ranch houses. Houses of that age often have original or patched plumbing, so a slow leak can run unseen inside a wall for a long time.

Adobe and other earthen walls absorb water and release it slowly. If one gets wet, tell the crew before drying begins, because the method and the equipment choice depend on the wall material. Plaster over old lath behaves differently again.

Signs of trouble in an older Springville home

Age shows up in a few predictable ways.

Watch for

  1. Rust-tinted water or low pressure. Aging supply lines may be failing at joints.
  2. Slow drains and gurgling. An old lateral can crack or collect roots.
  3. Peeling paint on a plaster wall. Moisture is coming through from inside.
  4. A damp crawl space or cellar. Check for standing water before it reaches framing.
  5. Musty smell near a bathroom. A tub seal or toilet flange may be leaking.

The sewer lateral test and what it means for you

The city Building Division requires a sewer lateral water test at inspection. For a buyer or someone remodeling, that test can reveal a cracked or leaking lateral before it fails. For anyone else, it is a reminder that the pipe between the house and the main may be decades old.

A backup through a basement drain is a Category 3 event, so cleanup involves removing porous materials and disinfecting what remains. Ask the city Building Division what the test requires for your project. Sewer or drain backup often needs an endorsement on your policy, so check yours.

Hobble Creek runoff and who clears what

Hobble Creek runs through town. The city says low flows let debris build up and cut the channel capacity, and that snowmelt in spring 2023 was high. It clears debris at bridges and irrigation take-outs but not private banks, so households along the creek need to clear their own frontage.

A tidy bank before runoff is the cheapest flood prevention you have. New FEMA flood maps matter too. The city planning commission held a July 2026 hearing on adopting new FIRMs for Utah County into city code, so check the current FEMA flood map for your address. Flood damage is usually excluded from a standard policy, and flood coverage takes 30 days to start.

Springville services, led by repair and cleanup

Because older homes often need materials replaced, water damage repair comes up early. After drying, it covers plaster, drywall, flooring and trim that cannot be saved. Water damage cleanup handles removal and sanitizing right after the leak, especially after a sewer lateral failure.

For a burst line with a lot of standing water, emergency water removal is the opening step. When the flood source is Hobble Creek or heavy runoff, flood damage restoration applies.

What drives a Springville bill

Each job turns on a few things:

  • Water category 1, 2 or 3
  • Affected area
  • Materials involved, including plaster and adobe
  • Drying days and equipment
  • Rebuild scope

Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood or sewer backup often needs separate coverage. Check your policy.
